# 🏠 SpaceFinder

**SpaceFinder** is a web-based platform that helps users easily search and find 11-month rental accommodations — perfect for students, working professionals, and temporary city dwellers. The goal is to provide a smooth, trustworthy, and location-based rental discovery experience.

---

## 🌟 Features

- 🔍 **Location-Based Search** – Search properties by city, locality, or pin code.
- 🕒 **11-Month Rentals Only** – Focused exclusively on temporary rental agreements (ideal for students and professionals).
- 📸 **Property Listings** – View photos, rent, features, and landlord contact info.
- 🧑‍💼 **Landlord Dashboard** – Property owners can log in and post/edit their listings.
- ✅ **Verified Listings** – Ensures quality control and avoids scams.
- 🔐 **Secure Login System** – Role-based access for admins, landlords, and users.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Works well on both desktop and mobile.
